# Executive summary — LP rebalancing strategy backtest

**Scope:** ETH/USDC, Uniswap V3 0.05 % pool on Base · ranges ±0.5 %…±15 % · initial capital 100…2500 € · 1 year of 1-minute prices (2025-11-01 → 2026-11-01; ETH −41 %, realised vol ≈ 61 %) · pool volume 13.6 M$/day (measured on-chain) · benchmark: just holding the same tokens (HODL). All figures are **% vs HODL** (negative = the strategy lost to simply holding). Generated by `backtest/executive.py`; assumptions and limits in `backtest/README.md`.

## 1. Bottom line

- **At today's pool volume (13.6 M$/day) the strategy does not beat holding in any variant**: 0 of 448 range × capital × variant × settings combinations were positive.
- **Best plain version (always LP): -15.8 %** (at ±15 %). **Best with schedule + volatility filter: -3.0 %** (at ±15 %). For ranges ±5 % and wider, staying out at the US open and in high volatility removes more than half of the loss; for ±0.5…±2 % it removes only ~20-50 %. It does not turn the result positive.
- **Initial capital (100 €…2500 €):** for the plain strategy it does not change the percentage for ranges ≥ ±5 %. The filtered variants make ~284 enter/exit transactions a year, so the fixed gas weighs on small accounts: at ±15 % variant D gives −8.6 % with 100 € but −2.7 % with 2500 € (section 3). Below ~500 € the extra transactions eat a good part of the benefit.
- **Narrow ranges (±0.5…±2 %) are the worst bet**; wider ones lose less. The 1-minute data show no sweet spot in the narrow zone.
- **Profitability depends on pool volume more than on any setting:** break-even is ≈ 20 M$/day for always-LP and slightly lower (≈ 17-18 M$/day) with the filters; the pool averaged 13.6 M$/day (section 5).

## 6. What this means

1. **Do not expect to beat holding with the current setup.** Treat any live test (e.g. 100-500 €) as validation of the machinery, not as an investment.
2. **Prefer wide ranges (±10…±15 %) and few rebalances** unless pool volume is persistently above ~20 M$/day; use the measured weekly volume (`python3 backtest/pool_volume.py 168`) as the go/no-go signal.
3. **If the strategy is automated further, staying out at set hours (US open) and in high volatility is the lever with the best, most consistent evidence** (sections 2 and 4) — implementation in the contract is in progress and needs your sign-off because it changes the strategy.
4. **Still open:** a gas-price gate (no historical gas data available), more than one year of history, and own-liquidity price impact.

_Caveats: one bear-market year · Binance prices as a proxy for pool prices · constant active liquidity · no MEV/JIT · gas assumed 0.02 $/tx._
